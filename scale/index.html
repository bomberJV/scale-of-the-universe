IN THE FRAME

    What is measured, what is drawn, and the sources

    Every size, distance and position is a real one, and the frame's width is always the number shown. What cannot be measured is drawn, and kept to the right size. Figures were checked against the sources below on 18 November 2026; the ones that were not looked up again are named as such.

    Below a millimetre the zoom closes on a droplet from a cough, a cell from the inside of a cheek in it, the cell’s nucleus and one nitrogen atom in its DNA. Measured: a cheek cell about 50 to 70 µm across with a nucleus about 8 to 9 µm (J. Cytology); nuclear pores 120 nm (BioNumbers); adenovirus about 90 nm, docking at a pore (J. Virol.); nucleosomes of 147 base pairs wound 1.65 turns, and the double helix 2 nm wide with 0.34 nm a base pair. From general reference: the droplet, the organelles, the bacteria and the other viruses. Where things sit in the cell, the shapes of the organelles and the 30 nm fibre, which is clearer in textbooks than in living cells, are drawn. At the bottom of the DNA the frame turns to look down the helix at one adenine–thymine pair: its atoms follow the standard base geometry (Olson et al., J. Mol. Biol. 313, 2001), set so that adenine’s N1 is 0.288 nm from thymine’s N3, the ideal B-DNA figure in 3DNA. The water molecule beside it has O–H bonds of 95.7 pm at 104.5° (Britannica). This part of the zoom is the same one red’s homepage shows, from the same file.

    The region. State and county outlines are the US Census Bureau’s 2023 cartographic boundary files at 1:500,000 (census.gov), simplified to about 90 m for counties and 250 m for states. Inland rivers such as the Hudson are inside those outlines, so from far out they show as land. The highlighted counties are the ones I work in. Landmark positions are from general reference, to about 100 m, and were not looked up again; the Empire State Building, One World Trade Center and the Statue of Liberty stand at their real heights, and every other building is drawn.

    Below the proton. The particles round the quark are every one in the Standard Model, with masses from the Particle Data Group (2024) and the neutrinos’ bound, under 0.45 eV, from KATRIN (2025); the graviton, the axion and the sterile neutrino are predicted, not seen, and drawn dashed. None has shown a size, so each is the same ball. No experiment has found a size for a quark: its radius is under 0.43 × 10⁻¹⁸ m (ZEUS Collaboration, 2016). The Planck length is 1.616255 × 10⁻³⁵ m (CODATA 2018, NIST). The reach of the weak force (about 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁸ m) and the length a 13.6 TeV collision reaches (about 1.5 × 10⁻²⁰ m) are worked out from the W boson's mass and the collision energy. Between there and the Planck length nothing has been measured, so only circles of each length are drawn.

    Small. A worker honeybee is 12 to 15 mm long (PBS Nature). A human hair is about 100 µm across and E. coli about 2 µm long (Microbe Online). An influenza virus is about 100 nm across (Charles Sturt University). The nitrogen molecule's bond is 109.76 pm and the atom's van der Waals radius 155 pm (Wikipedia, Nitrogen). The proton's charge radius is 0.84075 fm (CODATA 2022). Fine dust is PM2.5, under 2.5 µm by definition. Air is drawn with as many molecules as that volume holds at 20 °C and sea-level pressure (2.5 × 10²⁵ in a cubic metre, from the ideal gas law), 78.08% nitrogen, 20.95% oxygen and 0.93% argon. Not looked up again: pollen at about 20 µm and the nitrogen-14 nucleus at 2.56 fm in radius (Angeli and Marinova, 2013). Where each thing sits, and its shape, is drawn.

    New York and the Earth. Borough outlines are the Department of City Planning's; coastlines are Natural Earth's. Manhattan's grid is turned 29° from north with 80 m between streets and 280 m between avenues, carried over the whole island; these come from the red homepage's notes and were not looked up again. Over the city the camera is held in the middle of Seventh Avenue, looking up it, so nothing stands between it and the crossing. The buildings, their heights (20 to 245 m; Midtown's tallest are about this), their lit windows and the screens are drawn, as are the traffic, the people and what stands on the sidewalks (lamps, trees, signals, hydrants, kiosks, a newsstand, carts, a bus shelter, a subway entrance, a Citi Bike station, benches; a LinkNYC kiosk is 2.9 m tall, as CityBridge publishes); a city bus is 12 m long and a person about 1.7 m tall. The bubbles near the Planck length are how that scale is often pictured, not something measured.

    Out to the Milky Way. This is the homepage's pull back: the Moon and planets where they are today, the Kuiper belt and Oort cloud as populations, the catalogued stars at their measured places, and a model of the Milky Way built on its measured spiral arms.

    The Local Group. Directions and distances of all thirty galaxies are from table 2 of McConnachie (2012), The Astronomical Journal 144, 4. Discs are tilted as catalogued; the detail inside them is drawn.

    Nearby galaxies. Distances out to Centaurus A are from Wikipedia's list of nearest galaxies, and directions were checked against An Atlas of the Universe's list (all within 0.1°). Not looked up again: M83, M94, M64, M101, M51, M106, the Sombrero and M87, and every galaxy's size and tilt.

    Clusters. Positions are the Abell catalogue's (Wikipedia, List of Abell clusters). Distances are the ones the NASA/IPAC Extragalactic Database gives, as quoted on each cluster's Wikipedia page (for example Hydra, Norma and Leo), rounded to 10 million light-years. Sources disagree by 5 to 20 percent at this range: An Atlas of the Universe, working from redshift alone, puts Hydra at 158 million light-years against 190 here, and Centaurus at 142 against 170.

    The region. State and county outlines are the US Census Bureau’s 2023 cartographic boundary files at 1:500,000 (census.gov), simplified to about 90 m for counties and 250 m for states. Inland rivers such as the Hudson are inside those outlines, so from far out they show as land. The highlighted counties are the ones I work in. Landmark positions are from general reference, to about 100 m, and were not looked up again; the Empire State Building, One World Trade Center and the Statue of Liberty stand at their real heights, and every other building is drawn.

    Passing by. A rock pigeon, 30 to 36 cm long with wings 50 to 67 cm across, and a house sparrow, 16 to 17 cm (Cornell Lab); a ginkgo leaf, a fan 5 to 8 cm wide (Missouri Botanical Garden). Their shapes, colours and paths are drawn. In the cell, keratin filaments about 10 nm thick, a centrosome, lysosomes and microtubules 25 nm across; a kinesin walks one in 8 nm steps (Vale, 2003); how many and where are drawn.

    Vehicles. Each is drawn at one real model's size: a 2025 Toyota Camry for the sedan and the yellow cab (CarExpert), a Toyota RAV4 (Motor1), a Ford F-150 SuperCrew (iSeeCars), a Ford Transit high-roof van (Ford), a box truck with a 26-foot box (Penske; its cab drawn), a rear-loading garbage truck (Seattle Public Utilities), New Flyer Xcelsior 40- and 60-foot buses (Wikipedia) and a tractor-trailer at New York City's 55-foot limit (NYC DOT). Their shapes are drawn.

    Below the proton. No picture of a quark or a proton exists to copy. The proton is drawn after what lattice QCD computes: three quarks joined by one Y-shaped tube of gluon field that meets at a junction (Takahashi, 2004), drawn as a soft band; the quarks are points, since none has shown a size. The nucleus's nucleons are soft clouds, held together by pions passed between them. Below the reach of colliders, about 10⁻²⁰ m, nothing has been measured, so nothing is drawn: the frame keeps its scale down to the Planck length, which is built from measured constants (G, ħ and c). The lengths that only theories name (grand unification, the seesaw, strings) and pictures of the vacuum (particles flickering, spacetime foam) are left out.

    Superstructures. Laniakea, 520 million light-years across, with its middle about 250 million light-years away (Tully and others, 2014); its edge is drawn as a sphere, though the real one is uneven. The Perseus–Pisces Supercluster, about 300 million light-years long and 250 million away (Wikipedia). The CfA2 Great Wall (Geller and Huchra, 1989), drawn through the Leo, Coma and Hercules clusters it holds. The Sloan Great Wall, 1.37 billion light-years long about a billion away, from 8.7h to 13.7h along the celestial equator (Gott and others, 2005). The Boötes Void, about 62 Mpc in radius, its middle 700 million light-years away (Wikipedia). Their shapes are drawn; structures still argued over, such as the Hercules–Corona Borealis Great Wall, are left out.

    Farther. MoM-z14 is the farthest galaxy confirmed, at a redshift of 14.44 (Naidu and others, 2025); with the Planck 2018 cosmology that is 280 million years after the Big Bang and 33.9 billion light-years away today. JADES-GS-z14-0 has a redshift of 14.1796 (Schouws and others, 2025): 290 million years, 33.7 billion light-years. Quasar 3C 273 (redshift 0.158, not looked up again) is 2.2 billion light-years away on the same reckoning. The sky positions of all three were not looked up again. Past a billion light-years, distances are where a thing is now, not how long its light has travelled. The edge of the observable universe is drawn at 46.5 billion light-years, the usual figure; the same reckoning gives 45 to 46.

    The puppy. The beagle puppy on the crosswalk at Times Square is drawn about 30 cm tall, inside the 33 to 38 cm the AKC gives for grown beagles. His barks, the car horns, the note at each stop and the sound of each scene, from the shimmer near the Planck length to the hum at the edge of the universe, are made in the browser as you go and are invented to suit the scene, not recordings. The notes that open when you press a name are short summaries; each links to somewhere to read more.

    Along the bottom of the frame run wavelengths, drawn to its scale: kinds of light (c / f, or h c / E for a photon’s energy), each particle’s quantum wavelength (the Compton wavelength h / (m c), from masses in the Particle Data Group’s 2024 review; the neutrinos’ from KATRIN’s 2025 limit, so theirs is a least length), a few moving things’ de Broglie wavelengths h / (m v), and sound, water and gravitational waves. The lengths are measured or worked out from measured figures, from general reference; drawing each as a plain sine wave is drawing. Set down for scale, and not where they are drawn: the Titanic in Seventh Avenue (269.1 m long, 28.2 m in the beam, 53.3 m from keel to funnel tops; Encyclopedia Titanica) and three giant stars about the Sun (Rigel about 74 solar radii, Betelgeuse about 764, Stephenson 2-18 about 2,150, the last uncertain). Midtown’s tallest towers stand at their real places and heights (Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat); their shapes are plain blocks. These figures are from general reference and were not looked up again.
